The name-and-signature stage of the matcher now compares its score with the threshold in exact fractions. The score, `0.5 × member + 0.3 × signature + 0.2`, was summed in floating point, so a pair that scored exactly its row's threshold landed a rounding error to one side of it or the other. A method that moved file and gained a fourth name token with one of four parameters retyped (`createUserAccount(a: string, b: number, c: Date, d: boolean)` → `createUserAccountRecord(…, d: Options)`, exactly 0.85) scored `0.8499999999999999` and was reported as one `added` and one `removed`, tripping `--fail-on removed`, while other pairs at the same score rounded up and paired. Every term of the score is a ratio of two counts, so it is now kept as one fraction and compared with its row (1, 19/20 or 17/20) by cross-multiplying integers, and such a pair is one `moved+changed` with rationale `name-signature`. Two candidates whose scores are the same fraction now also tie, and the lower base id, then head id, settles them, where rounding used to choose.

The IR does not change, so no rescan is needed: two stored IRs compared with this release report these pairs as `moved+changed` where the previous release reported an addition and a removal. `signatureSimilarity` now returns its exact mean divided out once, which can differ from the previous value in the last bit; `jaccard`, `nameSimilarity` and `memberSimilarity` return what they did.
